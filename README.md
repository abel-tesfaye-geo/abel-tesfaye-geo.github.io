# 🌍 Geospatial Portfolio: Abel Tesfaye

**Geospatial Science · UAV Photogrammetry · Earth Observation · Geovisualisation**

I use UAVs, satellite imagery, GIS, and spatial analysis to turn complex geographic data into accurate, interactive geospatial information. I'm a gold-medalist geospatial engineer and Assistant Lecturer at Bahir Dar University's Institute of Land Administration, working on land administration and urban planning in Ethiopia.

🌐 **Live portfolio:** https://abel-tesfaye-geo.github.io/

---

## 🚀 Featured Projects

| Project | What it is | Links |
|---|---|---|
| **3D Digital Twin, Addis Ababa** | Interactive 3D digital twin of a site in Addis Ababa, built from DJI Mavic 3E aerial imagery processed in Agisoft Metashape and One3D. | [Portfolio](https://abel-tesfaye-geo.github.io/projects/) |
| **Bahir Dar Atmospheric Watch** | Interactive web map of column concentrations of seven trace gases over Bahir Dar, from Sentinel-5P TROPOMI data on Google Earth Engine. | [Live demo](https://abel-tesfaye-geo.github.io/GEE_GAS_DETECTION/) · [Repo](https://github.com/abel-tesfaye-geo/GEE_GAS_DETECTION) |
| **Bahir Dar Structural Plan Basemap** | Field-surveyed digital cadastre of Bahir Dar (parcels, roads, waterways, control points), digitized 2018–2020 and published as an interactive map. | [Live demo](https://abel-tesfaye-geo.github.io/structural-plan-BDR/) · [Repo](https://github.com/abel-tesfaye-geo/structural-plan-BDR) |
| **Urban Expansion & Land Cover, Bahir Dar** | Random Forest land-cover classification on Google Earth Engine to quantify urban spatial expansion. | [Interactive map](https://abel-tesfaye-geo.github.io/urban-expansion/) |
| **Tis Abay: Where the Blue Nile Smokes** | Scrollytelling story map on how upstream diversion reshapes the Blue Nile Falls. | [Story](https://arcg.is/1f8Oee3) |
